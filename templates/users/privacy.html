What we store

Resource Planner uses the following cookies and browser storage mechanisms:

Session cookie (sessionid)
Required. Identifies your authenticated session. Expires when you sign out or the browser closes.
CSRF cookie (csrftoken)
Required. Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks.
Active financial year cookie (rp_active_fy)
Functional. Caches the active financial year for instant navbar display on page load. Contains only public FY data (name and dates).
Active sprint cookie (rp_active_sprint)
Functional. Caches the active sprint for instant sprint pill display. Contains only the sprint name, dates, and remaining days.
Cookie consent (rp_cookie_consent in localStorage)
Functional. Stores whether you have acknowledged this notice, so the banner is not shown on every page.
Sidebar state (rp_main_collapsed, rp_sub_collapsed, etc. in localStorage)
Functional. Remembers your sidebar collapse preferences between pages.
What we do not store

No advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies are used. No data is shared with third parties.

Managing cookies

You can clear all cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. The application requires the session and CSRF cookies to function; clearing them will sign you out.
